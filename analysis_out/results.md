# Caveats and explanations (read with the sections above)

## A. Why A period 1 looks flat
- What the log shows: the start command arrived (setup header at 348 s, mode `breathing`, `motors on`), the
  board ran the normal phase sequence (10 INHALE / EXHALE phases of 4 s / 6 s, F lines) and drove the inflate
  pump at PWM 255 in every INHALE (P lines). The pressure still stayed at 0.0 kPa (max 0.09, min -0.04).
- At the stop (442 s) DEFLATE ran its full 20 s timeout (442-462 s), because the pressure never reached -1 kPa.
  Periods 2 and 3 ended DEFLATE in 1.2 s and 0.3 s.
- No fault, safety stop, restart or range error is logged. Same setup and same zero offset (0.61 kPa) in all three
  starts, so it is not a mode or setting difference.
- So the board commanded the pump but no air reached the sensor / bag: most likely a physical cause (tube not
  connected, or the pump's motor supply off). The log cannot tell which. A `pump:auto` marker at 463 s (the Auto
  button pressed right after that DEFLATE) suggests the operator noticed something; from period 2 on (519 s) the
  bag inflated normally.
- A's starts say "rise for 30 s": that session ran before the start rule was shortened to 15 s.

## B. Pressure
- The 3 kPa inhale target was never reached (0 of 58 cycles in B); the inhale phase always ran its full 4 s
  at PWM 255, so the bag on the body peaks at about 1.5 kPa. Cycle length is fixed by the firmware timer
  (4 s + 6 s = 10 s, 6 breaths/min), not by the pressure.

## C. Notes
- A period 1 is counted as a start although the bag did not inflate (see A).
- D: the pump stayed on 279 s until the end of the session, because the BITalino battery died (see D).
- 9 of the 10 starts fell during a task, 1 in a rest (B, 735 s, 2.5 min after math ended).
  4 sessions, 4 people; the demo sessions had no control block.

## D. Caveats
- D: the BITalino battery died; EDA and ACC are invalid from about 878 s (14.6 min) to the end (the
  `battery dead` note at 986 s was typed later). EDA from 878 s is treated as missing. **D's English (airbag)
  block is excluded from D and from any airbag-vs-control comparison.** Heart rate (RT7015) is valid for the whole
  session but is not used here. D's math (control) would-start at 547 s is kept.
- With D's airbag block excluded, only C_3 has both an airbag and a control block (1 pump start vs 3
  would-starts in one person). No paired airbag-vs-control comparison is possible.
- The rule starts the pump after EDA / arousal has risen, so EDA often falls afterwards on its own; the control
  would-starts are there to show this. Groups are small (8 vs 4 events, 3 vs 2 people), events within a person are
  not independent, and EDA levels differ a lot between people (hence the change from each event's own -10..0 s).
  Descriptive only.
- C_3's pump start (387 s) has the largest rise: +7.3 µS peak about 30 s after the start, during math.
- A period 1 (command sent, bag flat) is shown as a dashed line and kept out of both means.

## E. Caveats (E)
- One person, one session; the markers were corrected after the session (`markers_before_edit.csv` keeps the
  original).
- Step 1 (rest) came before the baseline (chat), so there is no arousal for it (0 valid seconds in the replay).
- Step 3 (rest) has no self-report: the recording stopped at 632 s, was paused about 14 min and resumed; step 4 is
  the rest after the resume. Its HR (93 bpm) and EDA are higher than in the other rests.
- The labels and the signals do not agree everywhere: math was rated 5 but has a mean arousal of 0.47; the game was
  rated 2 with the highest arousal (0.70).
  This is the kind of label noise that a learned model would have to handle.
- Arousal uses the hand-set default weights (not a learned model).
